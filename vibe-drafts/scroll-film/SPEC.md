- **Who I am:** Ines Duarte, second-year Cinema and Media Arts student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want production companies and festival programmers to see how I frame, cut and score a short film.
- **About me:** I think in shots. I like planning a scene on paper, shooting it with whatever light I can find, and finding the film again in the edit.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Night Shift: a four-minute short film about a late-night transit worker, shot over two weekends for a second-year production course. Link: https://example.com/ines-duarte/night-shift
  - Paper Weather: a two-minute stop-motion animation made from cut paper, a phone on a tripod and a desk lamp.
  - Rooftop Loop: a thirty-second looping motion piece, edited and graded for a class on moving image design.
- **Skills:** Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, cinematography, storyboarding, sound editing.
- **Contact:** ines.duarte@example.com; LinkedIn: https://example.com/ines-duarte.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-scroll-film`: cinematic, paced, art-directed.
