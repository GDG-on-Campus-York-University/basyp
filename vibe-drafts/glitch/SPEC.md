# Sample portfolio spec (gallery example, fictional student)

- **Who I am:** Priya Raman, second-year Digital Media student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want studios and internship teams to see how I use code, sound and motion to make short visual pieces.
- **About me:** I like building small pieces that move, react to sound or loop forever, then tweaking them until they feel right. I work in code first and finish in video editing software.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Static Bloom: a 30-second generative visuals piece made with p5.js, where thousands of tiny shapes grow and fade from a noise field.
  - Pulse Room: a music visualizer that turns a track I recorded into moving bars and rings in the browser. Link: https://example.com/priya-raman/pulse-room
  - Loop Study: a motion design assignment for my second-year course, a 10-second looping animation of a city street built in After Effects.
- **Skills:** p5.js, Processing, TouchDesigner, After Effects, Premiere Pro, Figma, Ableton Live, HTML and CSS.
- **Contact:** priya.raman@example.com; GitHub: https://example.com/priya-raman; Vimeo: https://example.com/priya-raman-reel.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-glitch`: clean, precise, with the occasional controlled glitch.
