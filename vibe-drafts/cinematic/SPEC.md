# Sample portfolio spec (gallery example, fictional student)

<!-- GALLERY SAMPLE. Tariq Bennett and these projects are made up for the vibe gallery. Use this file as the content source for this sample site only. -->

- **Who I am:** Tariq Bennett, third-year Cinema and Media Arts student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want festival volunteers, production companies and summer-job employers to see how I frame a shot, cut a scene and shape sound.
- **About me:** I like starting with one image or one sound and working out what a scene needs around it. I shoot on borrowed gear and phones, edit late in the lab, and care most about patience: holding a shot long enough for it to mean something.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Last Bus Home: a short narrative film about a late-night commute, made with classmates for a production course. I was the director and editor.
  - Window Light Study: a silent video exercise for a cinematography course that follows one room as the daylight changes, lit only with a window and a reflector.
  - Corner Store Portrait: a short documentary portrait of a neighbourhood shop owner in Toronto, with interview audio recorded on location and mixed in the lab.
- **Skills:** DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, colour grading, location sound recording, Audacity, shot lists and storyboarding, screenwriting.
- **Contact:** tariq.bennett@example.com; Vimeo: https://example.com/tariq-bennett.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-cinematic`: patient, visual, a little dramatic, like the opening of a film.
