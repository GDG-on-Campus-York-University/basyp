Third-year Cinema and Media Arts student at York University.

About

I like starting with one image or one sound and working out what a scene needs around it. I shoot on borrowed gear and phones, edit late in the lab, and care most about patience: holding a shot long enough for it to mean something.

Projects

  • Last Bus Home

    A short narrative film about a late-night commute, made with classmates for a production course. I was the director and editor.

  • Window Light Study

    A silent video exercise for a cinematography course that follows one room as the daylight changes, lit only with a window and a reflector.

  • Corner Store Portrait

    A short documentary portrait of a neighbourhood shop owner in Toronto, with interview audio recorded on location and mixed in the lab.
