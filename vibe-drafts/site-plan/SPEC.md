- **Who I am:** Adaeze Nwosu, second-year Civil Engineering student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want co-op employers at engineering and surveying firms to see that I work carefully, check my numbers and can explain how a structure or a site behaves.
- **About me:** I like how civil engineering starts on paper: a sketch, a hand calculation, then a model or a field measurement to see whether the real thing agrees. I enjoy fieldwork days with the level and the total station, and I like writing up what went wrong as much as what went right.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Truss Bridge Model: a balsa-wood truss bridge for my statics course. I worked out the member forces by hand, built the bridge, loaded it until it failed and wrote up where it broke and why.
  - Drainage Design Study: a stormwater layout for a small parking lot, done as a course assignment. I sketched the catchment, sized the pipes in a spreadsheet and checked the slopes by hand. Drainage study report: https://example.com/adaeze-nwosu/drainage-study
  - Campus Surveying Traverse: a closed traverse around a campus courtyard, measured with a total station in a small team and adjusted afterwards in a spreadsheet.
- **Skills:** AutoCAD, Excel, Python, hand calculations, level and total station use, technical drawing, balsa model building.
- **Contact:** adaeze.nwosu@example.com; LinkedIn: https://example.com/adaeze-nwosu.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-site-plan`: careful, grounded, exact.
