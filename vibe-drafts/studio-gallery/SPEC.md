# Sample portfolio spec (gallery example, fictional student)

<!-- GALLERY SAMPLE. Rosalind Abara and these projects are made up for the vibe gallery. Use this file as the content source for this sample site only. -->

- **Who I am:** Rosalind Abara, second-year Visual Arts student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want studio programs, small galleries and design employers to see how I draw, print and make things by hand.
- **About me:** I work slowly and in series. I start with a drawing or a painting, then see what happens when I print it, bind it or put it in motion.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Salt Marsh Studies: a series of watercolour paintings of one stretch of shoreline, painted at different times of day for a second-year painting course. Medium: watercolour on paper.
  - Quiet Hours: a small zine of illustrated diary pages, printed in two colours and bound by hand with a needle and thread. Medium: two-colour risograph print.
  - Night Street Loop: a short looping animation of a paper lantern drifting down an empty street, drawn frame by frame. Medium: digital frame-by-frame animation. Link: https://example.com/rosalind-abara/night-street-loop
- **Skills:** watercolour, drawing, risograph printing, bookbinding, Procreate, Adobe Illustrator, frame-by-frame animation.
- **Contact:** rosalind.abara@example.com; Instagram: https://example.com/rosalind-abara-draws; Portfolio PDF: https://example.com/rosalind-abara/portfolio.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-studio-gallery`: quiet, spacious, work hung like pieces in a small exhibition.
