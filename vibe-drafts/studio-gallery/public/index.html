About

I work slowly and in series. I start with a drawing or a painting, then see what happens when I print it, bind it or put it in motion.

Projects

    A series of watercolour paintings of one stretch of shoreline, painted at different times of day for a second-year painting course.

  • Quiet Hours

    two-colour risograph print

    A small zine of illustrated diary pages, printed in two colours and bound by hand with a needle and thread.

  • Night Street Loop

    digital frame-by-frame animation

    A short looping animation of a paper lantern drifting down an empty street, drawn frame by frame.
