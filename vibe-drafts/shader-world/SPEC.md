# Sample portfolio spec (gallery example, fictional student)

<!-- GALLERY SAMPLE. Zainab Rahimi and these projects are made up for the vibe gallery. Use this file as the content source for this sample site only. -->

- **Who I am:** Zainab Rahimi, third-year Computer Science student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want graphics and games teams to see how I turn maths into pictures that run in real time.
- **About me:** I like the moment a few lines of maths turn into light on a screen. I spend most weekends writing small shaders and renderers, then taking them apart to see why they look the way they do.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Weekend Ray Tracer: a small C++ ray tracer that draws spheres with shadows and reflections to an image file, built while following a free online tutorial and then extended with a checkerboard floor.
  - Shader Sketchbook: a page of short GLSL fragment shaders in the browser (flowing noise, gradients and water-like ripples), each small enough to read in one sitting. Link: https://example.com/zainab-rahimi/shader-sketchbook
  - Terrain Flyover: a C++ and OpenGL program that builds hilly terrain from noise and lets you fly a camera over it with the keyboard, made for a computer graphics course.
- **Skills:** C++, GLSL, WebGL, Three.js, Python, Git, linear algebra.
- **Contact:** zainab.rahimi@example.com; GitHub: https://example.com/zainab-rahimi; LinkedIn: https://example.com/zainab-rahimi-linkedin.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-shader-world`: calm, precise, luminous.
