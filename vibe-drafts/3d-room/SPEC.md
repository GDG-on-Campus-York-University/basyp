# Sample portfolio spec (gallery example, fictional student)

<!-- GALLERY SAMPLE. Imani Castellanos and these projects are made up for the vibe gallery. Use this file as the content source for this sample site only. -->

- **Who I am:** Imani Castellanos, second-year Digital Media student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want studio internship teams and design instructors to see how I plan, model and build small spaces and objects.
- **About me:** I like starting with a paper sketch, building the space in 3D, and then making a small physical version I can pick up and move around.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Corner Studio: a 1:20 foam-board model of a small corner studio, built for a first-year spatial design course.
  - Lantern Lamp: a table lamp modelled in Blender and 3D printed, redesigned twice after light tests with different shade shapes. Link: https://example.com/imani-castellanos/lantern-lamp
  - Hallway Walkthrough: a short real-time walkthrough of a hallway, modelled in Blender and viewed in the browser.
- **Skills:** Blender, SketchUp, Rhino, Figma, model making, hand sketching, HTML and CSS.
- **Contact:** imani.castellanos@example.com; LinkedIn: https://example.com/imani-castellanos; Instagram: https://example.com/imani-makes.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-3d-room`: warm, tidy, toy-like.
