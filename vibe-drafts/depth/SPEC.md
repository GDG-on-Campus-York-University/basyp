# Sample portfolio spec (gallery example, fictional student)

<!-- GALLERY SAMPLE. Mara Lindqvist and these projects are made up for the vibe gallery. Use this file as the content source for this sample site only. -->

- **Who I am:** Mara Lindqvist, second-year Design student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want studios and design internships to see how I build things in layers, from paper models to screens.
- **About me:** I like making a flat idea feel deep. I start with cut paper and a desk lamp, then carry what I learn about light and space into digital layouts.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Ravine Shadow Boxes: three layered cut-paper scenes of a ravine, each built from five sheets and photographed under one lamp, made for a second-year studio course. Link: https://example.com/mara-lindqvist/ravine-shadow-boxes
  - Library Wayfinding: a signage concept for one floor of a campus library, tested with a paper prototype and classmates who tried to find their way.
  - Fold Lamp: a lamp shade folded from a single sheet of paper, redesigned twice after I saw how it threw light on the wall.
- **Skills:** Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, paper modelling, sketching, photography.
- **Contact:** mara.lindqvist@example.com; LinkedIn: https://example.com/mara-lindqvist; Instagram: https://example.com/mara-makes.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-depth`: layered, tactile, calm.
