About

I like making a flat idea feel deep. I start with cut paper and a desk lamp, then carry what I learn about light and space into digital layouts.

Projects

  • Ravine Shadow Boxes

    Three layered cut-paper scenes of a ravine, each built from five sheets and photographed under one lamp, made for a second-year studio course.

  • Library Wayfinding

    A signage concept for one floor of a campus library, tested with a paper prototype and classmates who tried to find their way.

  • Fold Lamp

    A lamp shade folded from a single sheet of paper, redesigned twice after I saw how it threw light on the wall.
