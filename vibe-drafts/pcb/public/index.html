About

I like projects where code and hardware meet: choosing the parts, laying out the board, soldering it, and then working out why it does not behave. I am happiest with a multimeter in one hand and a datasheet open.

Projects

  • Logic Probe

    A pocket tool I built for my digital logic course. It shows whether a pin is high, low or floating using three LEDs, and it lets me check circuits without an oscilloscope.
