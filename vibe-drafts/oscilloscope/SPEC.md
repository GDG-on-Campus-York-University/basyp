# Sample portfolio spec (gallery example, fictional student)

<!-- GALLERY SAMPLE. Noor Haddad and these projects are made up for the vibe gallery. Use this file as the content source for this sample site only. -->

- **Who I am:** Noor Haddad, second-year Electrical Engineering student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want co-op employers to see that I build circuits, measure them and debug them until they behave.
- **About me:** I like starting with a schematic and a simulation, then building the circuit and checking on the bench whether it does what I predicted. When it does not, I get to find out why.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Bench Power Supply: an adjustable linear power supply with a current limit, built from a transformer, rectifier and voltage regulator, and checked with a multimeter and an oscilloscope.
  - Light-Seeking Rover: a small two-wheeled robot with two light sensors and an Arduino that steers toward the brightest light, debugged one wire at a time.
  - Active Filter Study: an op-amp low-pass filter for a circuits course, simulated in LTspice, built on a breadboard and compared against the simulation.
- **Skills:** LTspice, KiCad, Arduino, C, Python, MATLAB, soldering, oscilloscope and multimeter use.
- **Contact:** noor.haddad@example.com; LinkedIn: https://example.com/noor-haddad.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-oscilloscope`: measured, curious, hands-on.
