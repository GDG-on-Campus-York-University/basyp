Second-year Electrical Engineering student at York University. I build circuits, measure them and debug them until they behave.

About

I like starting with a schematic and a simulation, then building the circuit and checking on the bench whether it does what I predicted.

When it does not, I get to find out why.

Active Filter Study

An op-amp low-pass filter for a circuits course, simulated in LTspice, built on a breadboard and compared against the simulation.
