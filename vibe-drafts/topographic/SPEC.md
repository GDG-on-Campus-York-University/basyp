# Sample portfolio spec (gallery example, fictional student)

<!-- GALLERY SAMPLE. Idris Lindqvist and these projects are made up for the vibe gallery. Use this file as the content source for this sample site only. -->

- **Who I am:** Idris Lindqvist, second-year Geomatics Engineering student at York University.
- **Audience and goal:** I want co-op employers at surveying firms, municipalities and mapping companies to see that I collect field data carefully and turn it into clear maps.
- **About me:** I like the full trip from the field to the map: carrying the instrument outside, taking the measurements, then checking at a desk whether they agree. I am happiest when a messy set of points becomes a map that someone else can read.
- **Sections:** Home, About, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- **Projects:**
  - Campus Tree Map: a map of trees along one campus path, with positions recorded by walking the path with a phone GPS app and cleaned and styled in QGIS.
  - Closed Traverse Adjustment: a surveying field course project where our team measured a closed loop of angles and distances with a total station, then adjusted the loop in a spreadsheet and plotted it.
  - Land Cover from Satellite Imagery: a remote sensing course project that sorts a Sentinel-2 image into water, forest, fields and built-up areas and checks the result against the image by eye.
- **Skills:** QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, Python, spreadsheets, total station and level, GNSS field collection, AutoCAD.
- **Contact:** idris.lindqvist@example.com; GitHub: https://example.com/idris-lindqvist.
- **Vibe and feel:** `vibe-topographic`: patient, careful, outdoors-minded.
