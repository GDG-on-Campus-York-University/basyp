About

I like the full trip from the field to the map: carrying the instrument outside, taking the measurements, then checking at a desk whether they agree.

I am happiest when a messy set of points becomes a map that someone else can read.

Projects

Campus Tree Map

A map of trees along one campus path, with positions recorded by walking the path with a phone GPS app and cleaned and styled in QGIS.

Closed Traverse Adjustment

A surveying field course project where our team measured a closed loop of angles and distances with a total station, then adjusted the loop in a spreadsheet and plotted it.

Land Cover from Satellite Imagery

A remote sensing course project that sorts a Sentinel-2 image into water, forest, fields and built-up areas and checks the result against the image by eye.
